What theming doesn't do

Custom theme is a module that's off by default and is switched on per gym; without it the Appearance page isn't available. It only themes the member app, its loading screen and icon, and LINE chat cards. The staff app and the owner console always keep the platform look, so they're shown unchanged below. Status colors (warning, success, danger), grouped-list layout and wording stay the same. Dark mode keeps a black ground with a lifted brand color.

03Owner console

Same console, your settings.

The console looks identical for every gym. What changes is what you set inside it: the Appearance editor holds your values, its live preview shows your member app, and generated LINE rich menus use your brand.

About this page

A product walkthrough of TapOnline prepared by Quentin Wu for Queen City Grappling Club. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by QCGC. QCGC's name is used only to personalize this proposal.

Mock screens

Every phone and console on this page is an HTML/CSS recreation with sample data. Labels, states and rules come from the current TapOnline build. Amounts are shown as “NT$ —” because the product currently prices in TWD.

Accuracy

Nothing here is a promise of features that don't exist. Layout details are simplified; the real screens may space, group or word things slightly differently.
