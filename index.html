05The member side, step by step

From first tap to next stripe.

01

Find it. Book it.

The app opens on this week's classes. Before a member commits, the class page shows what booking will cost (for example, one class credit) and whether they still need to sign a waiver.

  • “Up next” card with a live countdown and a check-in window bar
  • See who's coming, grouped by belt
  • Add to calendar: .ics download or Google Calendar
  • Booking cancellations return the credit automatically
02

Sign once. Properly.

A three-step waiver (Details, Read, Agree and sign) with the consents a martial arts gym needs. A member who signs as a minor gets a separate guardian link, and can't book until the guardian has signed.

  • Typed or drawn signature, with the stroke trace stored
  • Optional front-camera recording, up to 30 seconds, while signing
  • Emergency contact captured on every signature
  • Photo-likeness consent can be required or optional, your choice
03

Show the card. Get on the mat.

Every member has a QR member card. Staff scan it, or members check themselves in from the app when the window opens. You choose. Members can also save the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

  • Self check-in can be turned on or off
  • Check-in opens a set number of minutes before class (1–1,440)
  • Card shows membership status, valid-until date, classes left and stripes
  • Reset a card and old QR copies stop working
04

Membership, in their pocket.

Members see their own expiry and remaining class credits, and they can renew on the same screen. Every change sits in a ledger: granted, adjusted, used, returned or refunded. Nobody has to message the front desk to ask.

  • Trial, intro and free plans (claim free)
  • “New members only” plans and per-member purchase limits
  • Coverage time, class credits, or both. Unlimited options too
  • Payment history and pending payments in one place
05

Watch the mat time add up.

Every check-in can award training minutes. Members see total hours, the last 30 days, a 52-week heatmap, weekly streaks and progress toward the next belt goal, if you choose to show it.

  • Belt and stripe history with dates
  • “You train most on Tue & Thu evenings”-style insight
  • Goals are display targets only. Promotions stay a coach's call
  • Show or hide training time and progression per gym

PaymentsModule · Payments

Every dollar gets a status.

Payments are requests with a lifecycle: pending, reviewing, paid, cancelled, expired or refunded. When a payment is marked paid, the membership it was for is granted automatically. When it's refunded, that membership is revoked automatically.

  • Accepted methods: cash, bank transfer, LINE Pay (merchant connection)
  • Member-declared transfers go to an approvals queue
  • Only the owner can confirm money received
  • Staff payment requests wait for owner approval
  • Manual terms: coverage extension or a new expiry date
  • Stuck LINE Pay requests surface after 15 minutes for reconciliation
  • Filters by status, member and category (membership, merchandise, other)
  • Due dates, transfer notes and reject reasons

For QCGCThat Venmo-or-Cash-App seminar flow becomes a request the member sees, a queue you clear, and an automatic record of who paid. (Which US card and wallet processors to support is on our fit-check list below.)

16Questions

FAQ

01Do members have to download an app?

No. The member app opens in a browser, and members can add it to their home screen with your icon and name. Device notifications work once it's installed to the home screen.

02Can it handle kids and guardians?

Yes. A member who signs as a minor gets a guardian consent link. Until the guardian signs, the member's status is “pending guardian consent” and they can't book. You store the guardian's name, relationship, phone and signature.

03Do belts promote automatically?

Never. Hour goals per rank are display targets only. Promotions are always awarded by hand. Changing a belt's award date re-sorts past training time into the right belt.

04What happens to credits when we cancel a class?

Cancelling a class notifies booked members (if that alert is on), and credits used by its bookings are returned. That's different from a member cancelling their own booking, which also returns the credit.

05Can coaches see everything?

No. Coaches have their own view of their assigned classes and a staff queue scoped to those classes. Confirming money received is owner-only, and owner access is a separate, confirmed grant.

06Can we use our Instagram for this?

You can connect Instagram and a Facebook Page, and new customer messages then show up in the TapOnline inbox with staff alerts. Meta must approve the connection first. Replies are sent from Instagram or Meta Business Suite, not from TapOnline.

07Can we switch modules off?

Yes, per gym. Turning a module off hides its pages, and also turns off any modules that depend on it. The product warns you first.

08What does it cost?

We haven't put prices on this page on purpose. Terms depend on the fit-check items above, and we'd rather agree them with you directly. See the next steps below.
