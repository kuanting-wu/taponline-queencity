01Meet TapOnline

One record. Every screen.

TapOnline is a gym operations platform with four connected views, all reading the same data. When a member books on their phone, the booking is already on your console, the coach's roster and the dashboard. Nothing to export, reconcile or retype after closing.

Members

Member app

  • Opens in the browser. No app-store download
  • Add to Home Screen with your icon and name
  • Classes, bookings, member card, membership, payments, store, waiver, training history
  • Optional device notifications for class changes
Owners

Owner console

  • Desktop console for every module
  • Dashboard, members, classes, plans, payments, store, waivers, settings
  • Toggle modules per gym
  • CSV export on table pages
Owners & coaches

Staff app

  • Mobile workspace that mirrors the owner pages
  • Home queue, walk-in check-in, card scanning, inbox
  • Coaches see a queue scoped to their own classes
  • Push alerts for staff requests
Coaches

Coach view

  • “My classes”: upcoming classes assigned to you
  • Rosters, attendance, class notes
  • Public coach profile with bio and links (Instagram, Facebook, website)
  • Coach recap posts in class threads

02Interactive · 17 modules

Turn on what you use.

Every module can be switched on or off for each gym, and a module that's off disappears from navigation. Dependencies are enforced: booking needs classes, and belt progression needs belts and training time. Try it. These toggles follow the product's real dependency rules and defaults.

Tap a module to switch it.

The defaults shown are the product's defaults for a new gym. Custom theme, Payments, Store, Messages and AI assistant are off until switched on.

03Member app · 23 pages

The member app, page by page.

What members see on their phone, in the order they meet it: sign in, join, home, card, classes, booking, training, membership, store, payments, waiver, settings and the LINE link. Use the state buttons under each phone to step through what the code renders: empty, pending, blocked, error. Phones show the custom QCGC theme in light mode; the comparison page shows the default look next to it.

M01Member app

Sign in

Entry screen · before the member app

Members never create a password. The entry screen sends them through LINE login and lands them in the right gym, using the gym's link or a gym search.

What it shows

  • Gym logo (from Appearance) and the sign-in prompt
  • “Continue with LINE” as the only sign-in method
  • A help line for when the LINE app doesn't open
  • “Choose another gym” to switch gyms

What you can do

  • Sign in with LINE in one tap
  • Search by gym name or gym code
  • Pick from matching gyms, then continue to that gym's app

States & edge cases

  • No match: “No gyms found. Check the name or code.”
  • Bad or old link: “Gym could not be loaded. Check your link or try again.”
  • LINE login failure shows a retry message
  • Inside LINE's own browser, sign-in is automatic

First visit · member claim link

A first-time LINE user isn't a member yet. They either create their own profile, or scan a login QR from staff to attach their LINE account to a member record the gym already has.

What you can do

  • Link this LINE account to an existing member with “Link account”
  • Arriving from a class link? After joining they go straight to the membership tab of the store

States & edge cases

  • Claim links expire; expired or used links say “Ask staff for a new link”
  • Name is required to join
  • Owners create claim QRs from the member's page (“Give login”)

M03Member app

My account

Me tab · member home

The member's home base. The member card sits on top, followed by anything they still need to do and links into every member page the gym has switched on.

What it shows

  • Member card face: gym name, member name and photo, membership state, valid-until date, classes remaining and current belt
  • “To do” group: unsigned waiver, open payments, pending guardian consent
  • My classes with upcoming count
  • Membership & payments links
  • Training summary: belt, stripes, hours toward the goal, last 30 days
  • A staff-entrance icon when the member is also a coach or owner

What you can do

  • Tap the card to open the scannable member card
  • Tap the card's edit control to change name, phone and photo (JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 1 MB)
  • Jump to waiver, payments, training, settings
  • Switch to the staff app (staff only)
  • Dismiss an important notice with “Got it”

States & edge cases

  • Card states: Active membership · Membership expired · No active membership · Membership suspended · Member archived
  • Rows only appear when their module is on
  • Each section shows its own retry if it fails to load, without blanking the page
  • Skeletons while data loads

M06Member app

Class page

Classes › any class

Each class has its own page. The owner chooses which blocks appear and in what order (Class info, Your booking, Class notes, Who's coming, Add to calendar, Class thread, Action button), and the page changes before, during and after class.

What it shows

  • Class info band: date, time, coach, length, who's booked
  • Your booking status
  • Class notes from the coach
  • Who's coming (roster count, belt dots)
  • Add to calendar (Google Calendar or a .ics download)
  • Class thread

What you can do

  • Book (“Uses one class credit” when the plan is credit-based)
  • Cancel a booking (the credit comes back)
  • Check in yourself when self check-in is on
  • Show the member card for staff to scan
  • Sign the waiver or buy a membership when that's what's blocking

States & edge cases

  • Check-in opens at {time} · Check-in for this class has closed
  • “This check-in was removed. Ask the gym to restore it.”
  • “A guardian must sign before you can book.”
  • “This class has been cancelled.”
  • Booking errors are shown inline next to the action

M07Member app

Class thread

Class page › Class thread

A small discussion board attached to each class. Coaches can pin a recap; members post and reply. The owner decides who can read it and when posting closes.

What it shows

  • Posts and replies, newest last
  • Coach's recap, pinned on top
  • “Edited”, “Hidden” and “Pinned” badges
  • “Showing the latest posts only” on long threads

What you can do

  • Write a post, reply to someone
  • Edit or delete your own post
  • Coaches: pin as recap, unpin, hide from members, show to members

States & edge cases

  • Readers: class participants or all members (owner setting)
  • Posting closes 1, 3 or 7 days after class, when the next class starts, or never
  • Deleting removes the post and its replies for everyone
  • “No posts yet.”

O19Owner console

Rule settings

Settings › Training time · Check-in · Booking · Class page · Belt progression · Waivers

The rules behind the member app, one settings page per module.

What it shows

  • Training time: default minutes per class; show to members or not
  • Check-in: self check-in from the app, Wallet cards, how many minutes before class check-in opens (1–1440), reset a member card
  • Booking: coach undo window for walk-in bookings (0 turns it off, max 1440)
  • Class page: presets (Minimal · Training · Community · Custom), block order, thread readers and when posting closes, preview before / live / after
  • Belt progression: an hour goal per rank, shown or hidden to members
  • Waivers: versions (Draft · Active · Retired), signing rules

What you can do

  • Change any rule; most take effect for future actions only
  • Activate a waiver version, retire one, or require everyone to re-sign (shows how many members are affected)

States & edge cases

  • Training-minute changes apply to future awards only
  • Belt goals never promote anyone automatically
  • Retired waiver versions can't be activated again
  • Resetting a member card stops old QR copies and Wallet passes working

07Questions

FAQ

01Do members have to download an app?

No. The member app opens in a browser, and members can add it to their home screen with your icon and name. Device notifications work once it's installed to the home screen.

02Can it handle kids and guardians?

Yes. A member who signs as a minor gets a guardian consent link. Until the guardian signs, the member's status is “pending guardian consent” and they can't book. You store the guardian's name, relationship, phone and signature.

03Do belts promote automatically?

Never. Hour goals per rank are display targets only. Promotions are always awarded by hand. Changing a belt's award date re-sorts past training time into the right belt.

04What happens to credits when we cancel a class?

Cancelling a class notifies booked members (if that alert is on), and credits used by its bookings are returned. That's different from a member cancelling their own booking, which also returns the credit.

05Can coaches see everything?

No. Coaches have their own view of their assigned classes and a staff queue scoped to those classes. Confirming money received is owner-only, and owner access is a separate, confirmed grant.

06Can we use our Instagram for this?

You can connect Instagram and a Facebook Page, and new customer messages then show up in the TapOnline inbox with staff alerts. Meta must approve the connection first. Replies are sent from Instagram or Meta Business Suite, not from TapOnline.

07Can we switch modules off?

Yes, per gym. Turning a module off hides its pages, and also turns off any modules that depend on it. The product warns you first.

08What does it cost?

We haven't put prices on this page on purpose. Terms depend on how your gym would set things up, for example sign-in, payment methods and currency, and we'd rather agree them with you directly. See the next steps below.

09What should we know before deciding?

The product is built around LINE: members sign in with LINE, and the chat booking, rich menu and broadcasts all run through a LINE Official Account. Prices are stored in TWD and class times are handled in Taipei time. Online payment is LINE Pay; cash and bank transfer use a declare-then-confirm flow. We'd go through each of these with you.
